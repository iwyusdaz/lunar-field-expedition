|---|---|---|---|

## Items checked and adjusted (honesty log)

- The full Davies & Colvin (2000) PDF returned HTTP 403; coordinates taken from
  search snippets quoting Table 1 are marked VERIFIED-SNIPPET and used only for
  approximate feature positions. The LM position is confirmed by two independent
  sources (Wikipedia, the-moon.us).
- Wikipedia's Apollo 15 article says Station 2 was "on the flank of Mons Hadley
  Delta"; the NASA history article says "on the slopes of Mt. Hadley". The app says
  "on the lower slopes of the mountain front (Hadley Delta)" — the delta attribution
  is the standard one in the site literature.
- Genesis Rock's exact age is genuinely uncertain across methods; the app never
  states a single number without "about" and the uncertainty is part of the Module 5
  uncertainty discussion.
- The Elbow position (26.02822°N) initially looked inconsistent with the mental image
  of the rille bend, but it reproduces NASA's stated ~3.2 km LM→Elbow drive distance,
  so it is accepted; the in-game rille position was additionally cross-checked
  against the DTM itself (see research/terrain-validation.md).
