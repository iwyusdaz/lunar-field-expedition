# Human testing protocol (templates, not yet used)

No sessions have been run. These templates exist so real classroom or playtest sessions can be added later.

## Consent
> You're invited to try a short science activity and tell us what was clear or confusing. It takes about 50 minutes. We are testing the activity, not you. You can stop at any time. We won't record your name with your answers. Screen activity is recorded only if you agree. Do you agree to take part? Do you agree to screen recording?

Participants under 18 need parent or guardian consent and school permission. Collect no personal data beyond what the session needs.

## Session script
1. Consent. Note device and browser.
2. "Please think aloud as you go." Start at Module 1.
3. Observe without helping; note hesitations, misreadings and questions.
4. After Module 3: "Why do you think the two crater counts came out the way they did?"
5. After Module 5: "What is your claim, and what evidence would change your mind?"
6. Close: easiest part, hardest part, anything that felt confusing or untrue.
