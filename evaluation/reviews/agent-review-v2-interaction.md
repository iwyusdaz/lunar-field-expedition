## DECISIONS #21 judgement

The locked decision 8 is "cinematic hotspot glide + free WASD/orbit". The build keeps the existing OrbitController for drag and arrows, uses WASD to pan the orbit target, and adds hotspot/prop glides. I judge this an acceptable reading. The decision asks for free orbit, which is kept, and for "optional WASD movement", which is delivered as ground-plane movement at a speed that scales with distance (0.6 × radius per second). A 3.8 m/s first-person walk would be unusable on a 12 km DTM, and the orbit camera is what every measurement tool and test relies on. What is missing compared with the decision wording is the "walks the camera there" option: there is no first-person stand-point. That is fine for this case. Record it as accepted in the review log.
